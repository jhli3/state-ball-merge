    Merge two of the same state to grow it into the next one up. How to Play, Ambient, Shape, Mode, and the state picker now live behind the ⚙️ Settings button, toggled open and closed with a click or the - button, so the left stick stays completely dedicated to aiming and Shake/Restart/Settings only fire from their own buttons (Y/+/-) — never from D-Pad/stick navigation. 🎮 D-Pad and the stick switch to moving the highlight (A to select, B to back out) only once you're inside Settings or the state picker. Works with a Switch Pro Controller or paired Joy-Cons over Bluetooth or USB. Toggle 🪐 Mode: Space to place marbles anywhere in the box instead of dropping them from the top — every marble pulls gently on every other one (harder the closer their sizes are) and everything drifts slowly back toward center, so the board naturally clusters and re-centers itself. Space mode is round-marble only.
